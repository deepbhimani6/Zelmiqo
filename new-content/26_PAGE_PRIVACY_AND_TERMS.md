Title:
Commitment to Data Privacy & Absolute Security

Your privacy and security are paramount. In accordance with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), Zelmiqo guarantees that:
1. 256-Bit Bank-Grade SSL Encryption: All transactions, card information, and customer credentials are encrypted end-to-end.
