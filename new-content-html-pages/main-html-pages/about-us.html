Our Story

Zelmiqo was founded with one clear purpose:To bring thoughtfully engineered, high-utility lifestyle devices directly to customers at honest, direct-to-consumer value.

We saw too many shoppers forced to choose between overpriced flagship gadgets and unreliable low-grade alternatives lacking after-sales support. Our goal became simple: partner directly with specialist manufacturers, curate only proven innovations, and back every order with responsive service.

Today, Zelmiqo serves households across Saudi Arabia, the Gulf, and international markets with reliable logistics and continuous product refinement.

To elevate everyday comfort and convenience through dependable smart devices, fair pricing, fast 7–8 days delivery, and transparent customer support.

Our Vision
