Comprehensive Warranty Protection. Zero Hassle.

At Zelmiqo, our commitment continues long after checkout. Every electronic gadget, wearable cooler, percussion massager, charger, and car appliance we sell is built for durable everyday performance.

This Warranty Policy applies across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and supported Gulf regions, giving you straightforward technical inspection, honest timelines, and fair repair or replacement support without punitive depreciation penalties.

The Zelmiqo Warranty Promise: Eligible electronic products carry our 1-Year Official Hardware Warranty against manufacturing defects, alongside our 7-Day Replacement Guarantee for out-of-the-box issues.

What Our Warranty Covers

Our warranty protects verified hardware and workmanship issues under normal household or personal use:

✓
Internal Electronics & Charging CoilsVerified malfunction in Qi wireless coils (such as 3-in-1 charging docks), USB-C power controllers, and motherboards.
✓
Motors, Fans & Compression PumpsAbnormal motor stoppage or mechanical failure in bladeless neck fans, fascia massage guns, mini coolers, and cordless car vacuums.
✓
Batteries & Power HoldingRechargeable batteries that fail to accept charge or lose normal operating stability shortly after purchase under standard charging.
✓
Digital Displays & Touch SensorsFaulty LED battery indicators, non-responsive touch controls, or temperature sensor malfunctions.

What Is Not Covered

Warranty coverage excludes damages caused by external accidents, misuse, or improper handling:

✕
Physical Drops & ImpactCracked casings, broken blades, crushed housings, or shattered screens from drops or heavy pressure.
✕
Water or Liquid DamageSubmersion in liquid or moisture entering motors, ports, or vents on non-waterproof electronics.
✕
Unauthorized Repair or ModificationDevices opened, dismantled, or altered by unauthorized third parties or fitted with non-original parts.
✕
Incorrect Chargers & Power SurgesDamage resulting from incompatible industrial high-voltage adapters exceeding manufacturer specifications.
✕
Normal Cosmetic WearRoutine surface scratches, silicone softening, or minor aging that does not affect core performance.
✕
Consumables & Lost AccessoriesDetachable sponge heads, filters, or cables damaged through routine wear.

Simple 4-Step Warranty Claim Process

  1. 1Submit Proof & Order Number: Email zelmiqo@gmail.com with your order number, serial details (if applicable), and a brief photo or short video demonstrating the issue.
  2. 2Pack Safely in Protective Boxing: Place the device with original accessories and cables in its box or sturdy padded packaging so it travels safely during transit.
  3. 3Technical Evaluation Within 7 Business Days: Once received at our service center, technicians inspect the unit within 7 business days to verify eligibility.
  4. 4Repair, Replacement & 7–8 Days Return Delivery: Approved claims receive a repair or replacement unit dispatched back via tracked delivery arriving in 7–8 days.

Packaging & Inspection Eligibility

Please ensure products sent for warranty inspection are properly cushioned to prevent transit breakage. Items found ineligible after testing will be returned cleanly without repair fees, and we inform you beforehand if any paid non-warranty service option is available.

Valid claims require proof of purchase within the active warranty period.

Service Delivery & Coordination

Technical inspection typically finishes within 7 business days of receipt (transit time in each direction follows our standard 7–8 days shipping window).

If a serviced or replacement item cannot be delivered on the first courier visit, our support desk contacts you promptly via phone or email to arrange convenient redelivery.

Helpful Warranty Reminders

  • ◆Warranty applies strictly to genuine products purchased directly through Zelmiqo within supported countries.
  • ◆Serviced or replacement units are delivered within the same supported country or region of original purchase.
  • ◆Always review the included manual and use standard certified 5V/2A or rated charging adapters for longevity.

وفي حال تعذر تسليم الجهاز بعد الصيانة أو الاستبدال، يتواصل فريق الدعم معك هاتفياً أو عبر البريد لتنسيق موعد تسليم بديل مريح.
