7-Day Golden Returns. Fast, Fair Refunds.

At Zelmiqo, we want you to feel completely confident ordering every smart lifestyle gadget. If your item arrives defective, damaged, or simply isn’t right for you, this page clearly explains how returns, exchanges, shipping costs, and refunds work.

Our 7-Day Golden Guarantee: You have a full 7 days from delivery to request a return or exchange. Verified faulty, damaged, or wrong items include 100% free return shipping and a refund within 7 business days after inspection.

How to Return or Exchange an Item

  1. 1Contact Us Within 7 Days of Delivery: Email zelmiqo@gmail.com with your order number and whether you prefer a refund or an exchange.
  2. 2Keep Packaging & Accessories Complete: Ensure the device is unused (unless defective upon opening) with its original product box, charging cables, manuals, and unpeeled protective seals intact.
  3. 3Receive Return Instructions or Waybill: Our team confirms eligibility promptly and emails your return courier instructions or prepaid shipping waybill.
  4. 4Inspection & Refund in 7 Business Days: Once your returned parcel reaches our facility and passes verification, your refund goes straight back to your original payment method within 7 business days.

Return Shipping & Exchanges

Faulty, Damaged or Wrong Items

Zelmiqo covers 100% of return shipping.

If an item arrives defective, transit-damaged, or different from your order, we pay full return freight and arrange a free replacement or complete refund.

Change of Mind & Model Exchanges

7-Day Unopened Returns & Swaps

Changed your mind or prefer another gadget model or finish? Return the unopened device within 7 days (customer covers return courier shipping), and we issue your refund or ship your replacement once received.

Refund Payment Methods

✓
Cards, Mada & Apple PayApproved refunds return directly to the original card or Apple Pay account within 7 business days of return receipt (bank posting times may vary slightly).
✓
Cash on Delivery OrdersFor paid Cash on Delivery returns, refunds are transferred cleanly to your verified bank account within 7 business days once received.
✓
Replacement Dispatch WindowApproved replacement items ship swiftly via tracked courier with standard 7–8 days delivery.

Non-Returnable Items & Damaged Arrivals

For hygiene, safety, and digital security reasons, the following cannot be returned once opened:

✕
Opened Skincare & Hygiene DevicesFacial cleansing brushes, pore vacuums, ultrasonic scrubbers, or scalp applicator combs once the sterile hygiene seal has been broken or used directly on skin/scalp.
✕
Digital Gift Cards & Final SaleDigital vouchers, gift cards, and clearance items explicitly marked as Final Sale at checkout.

Received a Damaged or Incorrect Item? Inspect your delivery upon arrival and email zelmiqo@gmail.com with your order number and clear photos of the parcel box, shipping label, and product within your 7-day window so we can resolve it promptly.
