Fast, Transparent Delivery in 7–8 Days.

At Zelmiqo, we treat every shipment with care from the moment your order is placed until it arrives at your doorstep. Because many of our products feature lithium batteries, precision motors, and smart electronics, every package is verified and securely padded before dispatch.

Our Delivery Standard: Orders across Saudi Arabia and our supported regions are delivered within 7–8 days, supported by trusted courier partners and digital tracking updates.

How Order Fulfillment Works

Every order follows a structured quality and dispatch workflow so your delivery arrives on schedule within 7–8 days:

  1. 1Order & Payment Verification: We confirm your order details and shipping address immediately via email confirmation.
  2. 2Device & Accessory Check: Our fulfillment team inspects seals, charging cables, manuals, and overall item condition.
  3. 3Protective Thermal Packaging: Electronics are cushioned in shock-resistant boxing built to protect delicate components in transit.
  4. 4Courier Handover & Live Tracking: Your parcel is dispatched via trusted carriers and your tracking link is emailed once activated.

Shipping Coverage & Delivery Time

Saudi Arabia & GCC Cities

Delivery Time: 7–8 Days

We deliver to Riyadh, Jeddah, Mecca, Medina, Dammam, Khobar, Abha, Tabuk, and surrounding cities and provinces across Saudi Arabia and Gulf destinations.

International Shipping Coverage

Tracked Worldwide Delivery: 7–8 Days

International destinations are fulfilled via vetted cross-border express partners. Regional availability and carrier selection are displayed automatically at checkout.

Tracking Your Order & Address Assistance

✓
Real-Time Order TrackingOnce dispatched, use the tracking number in your shipping email or visit our Shipping Tracking page to follow delivery progress.
✓
Incorrect or Incomplete AddressPlease verify your street, district, building number, and active phone before confirming checkout. If you notice an error, email zelmiqo@gmail.com immediately so we can update it before dispatch.
✓
Missed Delivery & RedeliveryPlease ensure your phone is reachable when the courier calls. If an attempt is missed due to unavailability or unreachable details, contact support so we can help coordinate redelivery with the carrier.
✓
Damaged or Missing ParcelsInspect your parcel upon receipt. If anything arrives damaged or missing items, email photos of the box, shipping label, and product within the 7-day window so we can arrange a prompt replacement or resolution.

Delivery Responsibility & Support

Once a shipment is successfully delivered to the verified address provided at checkout, responsibility for the parcel passes to the recipient. Should severe weather, public holidays, or customs clearance create an unexpected transit hold, our team stays ready to assist you directly with the courier.

كيف يتم تجهيز وشحن طلبك؟

يمر كل طلب بأربع خطوات دقيقة لضمان الجودة ووصول الشحنة في موعدها خلال 7–8 أيام:

  1. 1التحقق من الطلب والدفع: يتم تأكيد تفاصيل طلبك وعنوان التوصيل فوراً عبر رسالة التأكيد.
  2. 2فحص الجهاز والملحقات: يتحقق فريق التجهيز من سلامة المنتج، كابلات الشحن، الكتيبات، والأختام الأصلية.
  3. 3التغليف الآمن المقاوم للحرارة: تُغلف الإلكترونيات بمواد عازلة ومبطنة تحميها من الصدمات وظروف النقل.
  4. 4التسليم لشركة الشحن والتتبع: تُسلم الشحنة للناقل المعتمد ويُرسل لك رابط ورقم التتبع فور تفعيله.
