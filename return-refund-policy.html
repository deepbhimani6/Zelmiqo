At Zelmiqo, we want you to be completely satisfied with every purchase. This Refund & Return Policy explains the terms, conditions, and procedures for returning, exchanging, or requesting a refund for products purchased from our store.

This policy applies to products purchased within multiple regions including the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, and other Gulf countries (“the Region”). Return and refund eligibility may vary depending on the product condition and category.

General Return & Refund Provisions

  • Customers may request a return, exchange, or refund within 7 days from the date of delivery.
  • Depending on eligibility and inspection, customers may receive a full refund to their original payment method or a replacement product.
  • To qualify for a return, the item must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition in which it was received, with all original tags, manuals, accessories, and original packaging intact.
  • To initiate a return or refund request, customers must provide valid proof of purchase and their order number.
  • Return requests submitted after the 7-day return window has passed will not be eligible for a return, exchange, or refund.

How to Request a Return

  • Start your return within 7 days of delivery by emailing zelmiqo@gmail.com or calling 6359268709 with your order number and the reason for your request.
  • Ensure the item is unused and securely packed in its original packaging with all tags, cables, and accessories included.
  • Once we review and confirm your return request, our team will provide return instructions and tell you where to send the item.
  • Once the returned item reaches us and passes inspection, we will process your refund or ship your replacement within 7 days.

Return Shipping & Responsibility

  • Zelmiqo covers return shipping costs when an item arrives faulty, damaged, defective, or wrong.
  • For returns due to personal preference or non-defective reasons, the customer is responsible for paying the return shipping cost.
  • Customers must ensure that returned products are properly packaged in their original packaging or suitable protective packaging during transit. We are not responsible for damage caused due to improper packaging during return shipping.

Refund Processing Policy

  • Once the returned item is received and verified at our warehouse, approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method within 7 days.
  • Depending on your bank or card issuer, additional processing time may apply before the refunded amount appears on your statement.
  • Original shipping fees and customer-paid return shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect.

Exchanges & Replacements

If you need a different variant, size, or color, you may request an exchange within 7 days of delivery using the same process as starting a return.

We will confirm product availability and ship the replacement as soon as the original item reaches us and completes inspection.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return or refund:

  • Gift cards and store vouchers
  • Items marked as final sale
  • Products returned after the 7-day return window
  • Used, altered, or incomplete items missing original tags, accessories, or packaging
  • Items damaged due to misuse, negligence, liquid exposure, or improper return packaging

Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or wrong, please contact us as soon as possible within 7 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos or a brief video of the issue so we can sort it out promptly.

Once verified, we will cover the return shipping and arrange a prompt replacement or full refund.
